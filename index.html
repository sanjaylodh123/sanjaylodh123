Solid Waste Management System of Dhangadhi Sub-Metropolitan City

Dhangadhi Sub-Metropolitan City is experiencing rapid urban growth, resulting in increased municipal solid waste. Lack of effective segregation and collection challenges are affecting environmental quality and public health.

Door-to-door collection occurs mainly in core urban areas, while peripheral zones remain underserved. Mixed waste disposal reduces recycling potential and increases landfill dependency.

A sustainable system requires source segregation, composting of organic waste, and recycling initiatives. Institutional capacity building, public awareness, and private sector participation are crucial for long-term success.

With proper planning, Dhangadhi can transform solid waste management into an environmentally responsible, economically feasible, and socially beneficial system.
